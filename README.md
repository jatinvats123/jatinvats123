### 👋 About

I'm **Jatin**, an AI engineer who ships **LLM agents end to end**: tool calling, guardrails, evals, and the full-stack product around them. I like voice AI that works on real roads and real phone lines, and agents that have to **prove** what they did before anyone trusts them.

- 💼 **Technology & Product Intern** at **WavyGo Mobility**: ERP backend, cloud, and AI agents on ops data
- 🎙️ Building voice agents in **Hindi, English and Hinglish**
- 🧪 Every agent I ship comes with **evals in CI**, not vibes
